Lasers

LASER equipment and materials are labeled in RED.

Important Information

*LASERS usage is separate from general BTU access and REQUIRES AN ORIENTATION.*

The Laser Cutter Orientation schedule can be found on the BTU Lab Calendar.

Anyone can gain access to operate the laser cutter using the process below.

Special Notes

FACULTY: Class assignments requiring laser cutting should be facilitated by LAs or TAs, or instructors.

STUDENTS: Assistance with laser cutting for class assignments should be facilitated by class LAs or TAs first, then "Lazor Wizards", if available.

EVERYBODY: Laser cutter access is separate from general BTU access and REQUIRES AN ORIENTATION.

To gain full independent access:

  1. Complete the laser cutter orientation. The schedule is on the BTU Lab Calendar.
  2. Class assignments where you need assistance are facilitated by LAs, TAs, and instructors first, then "Lazor Wizards", if available.
  3. As long as you need assistance, schedule laser appointments with a BTU Lazor Wizard listed below and on the BTU Mischief Makers page.
  4. Lazor Wizards provide "guided access". We will help you as much, or as little as needed.
  5. When you demonstrate independent and proper use of the laser cutter, you will have full access.
  6. Block 1-hour times on the whiteboard next to each laser. (Not during scheduled orientations.)

Laser Cutting Equipment & Materials

  • 75 Watt CO2 Laser, 30" x 18" bed size (762mm x 457mm)
  • 75 Watt CO2 Laser, 40" x 28" bed size (1016mm x 711mm)
  • Various Plywood
  • Various Plastics
  • Various Cardboard / Papers
  • Various Fabrics
